Hand Cricket

Test your cricket skills and luck in this digital version of the popular playground game!

How to Play

Choose a number from 1 to 6 using the buttons. If your number matches the computer's choice while you're batting, you're out! Otherwise, you score the number you chose. When bowling, if your number matches the computer's choice, they're out! Otherwise, they score their number.
